# IssueAI benchmark results — July 27, 2026

## Scope

- Dataset: 20 real historical issue cases from mature public repositories
- Evaluation question: are all expected real mechanisms present inside the top 20 ranked hypotheses?
- Observed result: 20/20 cases passed

## Stronger observation

In the measured run used to document the project, the expected mechanisms also landed inside the top 10 for all 20 cases.

## Diagnostic fallback

`top100_all` is kept only as a diagnostic envelope: if a future case misses the
top 20, it tells us whether the real mechanism was still retrieved somewhere in
the broader shortlist.

## What this proves

- IssueAI is already strong at high-recall hypothesis generation.
- The system is already good at not losing the real issue.
- The architecture is strong enough to justify a standalone project direction.

## What this does not prove

- perfect top-3 ordering
- perfect ranking purity
- full productized probe validation
- generalization to every possible repository shape

## Most important remaining weakness

Ranking noise still appears among correlated mechanism families, especially:

- representation
- contract
- precedence
- concurrency

The next benchmark phase should preserve 20/20 top-20 success while improving top-5 and top-3 precision.
